I am a Ph.D. student at Johns Hopkins University, advised by [Dr. Ben Van Durme](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~vandurme/). My research focuses on multimodal retrieval and understanding, specifically advancing efficient and scalable search over large multimodal collections and end-to-end reasoning and synthesis over retrieved multimodal content.


My work spans:
- **Efficient multimodal retrieval** with [late interaction models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19009v1) and [multi-vector index compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21202).
- **Complex and large scale retrieval** over video collections in [multilingual real-world settings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.11619) and with [complex user needs](https://nlp.jhu.edu/magmar/).
- **Multimodal RAG and generation** generating and grounding text in retrieved multimodal content, including [mutli-video](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00939), [cross-modal settings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05267), and [under uncertainty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08701).
- **Evaluation** of retrieval, generation, and RAG systems, introducing [metrics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24870) and [interpreting relationships](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08819).

My keywords are: multimodal retrieval, search, video understanding, multimodal RAG, efficiency.

Before Johns Hopkins, I got my B.S. from the University of Rochester advised by [Dr. Jiebo Luo](https://www.cs.rochester.edu/u/jluo/) and [Dr. Aaron Steven White](https://aaronstevenwhite.io/).
