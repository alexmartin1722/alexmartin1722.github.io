I am a Ph.D. student at Johns Hopkins University, advised by [Dr. Ben Van Durme](https://www.cs.jhu.edu/~vandurme/). My research focuses on multimodal retrieval and understanding, specifically advancing efficienct and scalable collaborative multimodal search and reasoning.


The problems I'm interested in are:
- **Efficient and scalable multimodal retrieval** with [late interaction models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19009v1), [multi-vector index compression](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21202), and rerankers ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02444),[2](finerank)) over [large-scale multilingual video collections]() that model complex user needs ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12295),[2](mvent-raw)).
- **Collaborative article generation** in multimodal query-driven article generation ([1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00939),[2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.12295)) and [interpretable continual document authoring](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26634).
- **Evaluations** for [text generation from any modality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.24870) and [interpreting RAG evals](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.08819). 

My keywords are: multimodal retrieval, search, video understanding, multimodal RAG, efficiency, collaborative generation.

Before Johns Hopkins, I got my B.S. from the University of Rochester advised by [Dr. Jiebo Luo](https://www.cs.rochester.edu/u/jluo/) and [Dr. Aaron Steven White](https://aaronstevenwhite.io/).
